OPNsense Firewall & Network

Virtual firewall/router used to experiment with routing, DHCP, DNS, IDS/IPS and multi-gig networking.

Overview

The OPNsense VM sits on the Dell T5810 and handles the routing/security layer. The lab has included interface and bridge configuration, DHCP/DNS work, monitoring and IDS/IPS testing while troubleshooting connectivity between the ISP, firewall, router and Proxmox management path.
