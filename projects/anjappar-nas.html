CLIENT PROJECT

NAS Migration

Restaurant infrastructure project covering NAS replacement, storage setup, data migration and validation of the new shared-storage environment.

SynologyNASData MigrationClient Infrastructure

Overview

The project involved replacing an existing NAS environment for an external client and moving the organisation onto a newer Synology-based storage setup. The work covered planning the replacement, preparing the NAS, migrating data, recreating access and validating that users could continue working from the shared storage.

What I worked on

  • Reviewed the existing storage setup and requirements.
  • Prepared the replacement NAS and storage configuration.
  • Planned and performed the data migration.
  • Recreated the required shared folders, access and permissions.
  • Validated connectivity and access after the migration.
  • Worked through the practical issues that appear during a real client-side infrastructure change.

What I learned

Replacing a NAS is not simply a matter of copying files. The project reinforced the importance of inventorying the existing environment, understanding how users actually depend on shared storage, planning downtime and verifying permissions and access after the cutover.
