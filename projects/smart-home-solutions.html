Overview

Local Smart Home Solutions is an independent service concept built around Home Assistant and local-first smart-home technology. The goal is to provide a practical way to integrate devices from different manufacturers without requiring customers to remain within a single proprietary ecosystem.

The project is being developed alongside my own Home Assistant homelab, allowing the service concept to be shaped around real-world installation, integration and troubleshooting experience.

Documentation

Concept

Local Smart Home Solutions is a Singapore-focused service concept built around Home Assistant. The goal is to provide locally controlled automation that can combine devices from different manufacturers instead of requiring customers to stay within a single ecosystem.

Service Scope

  • Home Assistant installation and configuration.
  • Smart dashboard creation and customisation.
  • Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter and Wi-Fi device integration.
  • Smart lighting and sensor automation.
  • Camera and security-device integration.
  • Energy monitoring and home automation.
  • Remote access setup, troubleshooting and optimisation.

Local-First Approach

The service concept prioritises local processing and control where practical. Home Assistant provides a common platform for integrating different brands while reducing dependence on individual proprietary ecosystems and cloud services.

Target Environment

The concept is designed around practical residential deployments, particularly HDB flats, condominiums and landed homes, with the potential to extend the same approach to small offices.

Building the Service

The project is being developed alongside my own Home Assistant homelab, allowing me to test integrations, dashboards, networking and troubleshooting workflows before applying the experience to client environments.

Current Focus

Current work is focused on refining the service offering, testing different device integrations and developing a practical approach to installation, configuration, troubleshooting and ongoing support.

The project is intentionally being developed incrementally so that the eventual service can be based on equipment and workflows that have already been tested in a real Home Assistant environment.

Current Status

The service is currently in the building and validation stage, with ongoing work focused on defining the service offering, testing integrations and developing a practical deployment approach.

Lessons Learned So Far

  • A local-first platform can bring devices from multiple manufacturers into a single smart-home environment.
  • Real-world testing is important before standardising devices or installation workflows for future client deployments.
  • A smart-home service involves more than device installation; networking, dashboards, troubleshooting and ongoing support are also part of the overall solution.
  • Keeping the service modular makes it easier to adapt the solution to different homes, budgets and device combinations.

This is an independent personal project and service concept. No confidential client information is published here.
